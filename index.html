Badges show where every photo lives

Each thumbnail carries tiny badges for the places it exists — this device, Dropbox, OneDrive, Google Photos. One glance tells you what's safe and what's stranded on a single copy, and a backup-status filter shows only the photos missing from a cloud.

Clean up

Reclaim gigabytes of duplicates

Byte-level fingerprints plus perceptual matching recognize the same photo even when a cloud re-encoded it. Review every group and pick where each copy should live — keep the clouds, free the phone, or the other way around.

  • One-tap presets: "keep on clouds", "keep on device"
  • A photo's last copy can never be deleted
  • Removed photos stay in the trash for ~30 days
Back up

Fill the gaps in your backups

Photomingo counts exactly how many photos are missing from each cloud and uploads them straight from the app — plus a one-time Google Photos import that brings your library down to your device, skipping anything you already have.

Stay in control

Filter by source or folder

Slice the gallery any way you like — only Dropbox, only your camera folder, or everything that isn't backed up anywhere yet.

Private by design

Your photos and their index never leave your device. Photomingo talks directly to your own Dropbox, OneDrive and Google accounts — there is no Photomingo server, no analytics, and no account to create. Cloud access tokens are stored encrypted on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
